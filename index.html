QC Brand Audit
QC Campaign Intelligence — FMCG Brands
Freemium · Live

FMCG brand managers on quick commerce platforms — Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart — spend hours trying to decode whether their ad spend is working. QC Brand Audit reads your platform CSV exports, scores campaigns against proven FMCG benchmarks on RoAS, POAS, and CTR, and delivers plain-English verdicts with STRONG / WATCH / PAUSE signals. Built for brand managers, not data analysts.

  • Parses Blinkit, Zepto, and Swiggy campaign exports automatically
  • Scores RoAS, POAS, CTR against FMCG-specific benchmarks
  • Plain-English STRONG / WATCH / PAUSE verdicts per campaign
  • AI-powered insights with actionable recommendations
  • Built for brand managers — no data analyst required

Final Journey
Post-Death Admin Checklist — India
Free · Multilingual · PWA

When a loved one passes, families in India face an overwhelming sequence of administrative tasks across banks, government offices, insurance companies, and courts — often in grief, often without guidance. Final Journey generates a personalised, step-by-step checklist across 3 phases and 17 task categories, available in 9 Indian languages. It works offline, respects your privacy (no data is stored), and costs nothing.

  • Covers 17 task categories across 3 time phases (immediate, week 1, month 1)
  • Available in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati & English
  • Personalised by deceased's details — ownership, family type, retirement status
  • Works offline as a Progressive Web App — no app store needed
  • Zero data collection — everything stays on your device

PaisaTrack
Personal Finance for Indian Earners
Freemium · No Login · Private

Most expense tracker apps are built for Western spending patterns — subscriptions, rent, credit cards. PaisaTrack is designed for Indian financial life: cash transactions, UPI payments, household expenses, EMIs, family remittances, and variable incomes. Track where your money goes, set category budgets, and understand your spending patterns — without handing your data to anyone.

  • Indian spending categories — groceries, EMIs, rent, family, festivals
  • UPI, cash, and bank transfer — all tracked simply
  • Monthly budget setting with visual progress tracking
  • No signup, no cloud sync — your data stays on your device
  • Works in Hindi and English

Dharani Group
Wood-Pressed Oil Business Manager
Live · Private · Role-Based

Built specifically for Dharani Group's wood-pressed oil operations, this business management app brings structure and visibility to a growing traditional manufacturing business. From production tracking and inventory management to customer orders and financial summaries — all in one responsive web app accessible from any device, with no costly ERP software required.

  • 10 functional modules covering the full oil production cycle
  • Role-based PIN login — admin, staff, and owner access levels
  • Google Sheets backend — no server costs, easy data access
  • Production batches, raw materials, and finished stock tracking
  • Customer orders, payments, and dispatch management

How We Build

Useful first. Everything else second.

We don't build apps to win awards or raise funding. We build them because something useful didn't exist yet.

01

Start with a real gap

Every AppQuest product starts with a genuine problem we've encountered — in our own lives or those of people around us. If we can't find an existing solution that's good enough, we build one.

02

Free by default

Most of our apps are free with no paywalls, no subscriptions, and no upselling. Where we do charge, it's transparent, fair, and because the product genuinely needs it to survive.

03

Privacy without compromise

We collect only what we need to make the app work. Most of our apps store nothing on our servers. Your data is yours — not an asset we're monetising in the background.

About AppQuest

One builder.
Many useful things.

AppQuest is an independent app studio based in Hyderabad, India. It's a one-person operation with a simple brief: find problems that real people have, and build the simplest digital tool that actually solves them.

The apps here are built across domains — family administration, business operations, brand analytics, personal finance — but they share a common thread: they were all built because the right tool didn't exist yet, or the existing tools were too expensive, too complex, or simply not built for Indian contexts.

AppQuest is not backed by investors, not chasing growth metrics, and not trying to be an aggregator. It's a small, focused studio that builds things that work and gives them to people who need them.
